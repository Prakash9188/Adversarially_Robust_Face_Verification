\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}
\paragraph{Data and protocol.} We use LFW (funneled)~\cite{huang2007lfw}, which has 13{,}233 images of 5{,}749 people. We split the \emph{people} (not the images) 70/15/15 into train, validation and test, so no person appears in two splits; the code checks this with an assert. Inside each split we sample an equal number of genuine and impostor pairs with a fixed seed (400/600/600 pairs). We did not use the official 10-fold LFW pairs because they mix people across folds, and after removing the overlap very few impostor pairs would be left for testing. Each image is center-cropped to 150\,px and resized to $160{\times}160$. We do not run a face detector or align the faces, which probably costs us a little clean accuracy.

\paragraph{Model.} We use a frozen InceptionResnetV1~\cite{szegedy2017inception} pretrained on VGGFace2~\cite{cao2018vggface2}, taken from the \texttt{facenet-pytorch} package~\cite{esler2019facenetpytorch}. We did not train it at all. We also did not check whether some people appear in both VGGFace2 and LFW.

\paragraph{Attacks and budgets.} $\epsilon\in\{2,4,8\}/255$, with the main results at 4. PGD: 10 steps, step size $2.5\epsilon/10$, random start. DeepFool: 10 steps, overshoot 0.02. CW: 30 Adam steps, $c{=}20$, $\kappa{=}0.05$. Square: 100 queries. The baselines use the first 300 test pairs, and the slow adaptive attacks use the first 150. Every attack changes only the probe image.

\paragraph{Defenses and metrics.} The filters are JPEG (quality 75/50/30), Gaussian blur ($\sigma{=}1$, $5{\times}5$), median ($3{\times}3$, $5{\times}5$) and 4-bit depth. At test time each filter is applied to \emph{both} images of the pair, and $\tau$ is picked again for each filter on validation pairs. We report accuracy, AUC, attack success rate (ASR), detector AUC and flag rate. ASR is the share of pairs that were correct before the attack and wrong after it. For our full system, an attacked pair only counts as a success if the answer is wrong \emph{and} the pair was not flagged.

\paragraph{Detector training.} The detector is trained on 200 pairs from the train split: clean probes (copied twice to balance the classes) against FGSM and PGD probes at $\epsilon{=}4$. We pick the detector type by its AUC on 200 validation pairs with the same two attacks. DeepFool, CW, Square, the other budgets and all adaptive attacks are never shown to the detector during training (\emph{unseen}).
